# LAMPLIGHTER — Official Rules (v1)

*A different game, for proving the agent reads its rulebook.*

---

## §1 Components

- 40 **Lantern cards** in two suits — Oil, Wick — values 1 to 4
- 8 **Post tiles**, numbered 1 to 8
- 1 **Warden token**

## §2 Setup

§2.1 Shuffle the Lantern cards. Deal nine cards to each player as their starting
**Satchel**.

§2.2 Place four Post tiles face up in a row. Return the rest to the box.

## §3 Turns

§3.1 On your turn, take exactly four actions. You may repeat an action freely.

§3.2 After your actions, if your Satchel contains more than eleven cards, discard down
to eleven.

## §4 Scoring

§4.1 When a Lantern card is played to a Post, score it at twice its face value.

§4.2 A player who lights three or more Posts in a single turn scores a Beacon Bonus of
7 points.
